Appendix D

Proof KPI library.

Efficiency
  • Time per task (hours or minutes per obligation, memo, or cycle)
  • FTE hours per cycle (full process hours, averaged over measurement period)
  • Throughput (number of items processed per period with same team)
  • Backlog reduction (outstanding items cleared vs. baseline)
Quality
  • Error rate (% of outputs requiring correction before use)
  • Rework rate (% of tasks returned for revision)
  • Completeness score (% of required fields or sections present)
  • Audit finding rate (findings attributable to the process in scope)
Control and evidence
  • Evidence rate (% of controls with automated, timestamped evidence)
  • Testing coverage (% of controls tested within required frequency)
  • Finding rate (internal audit findings in scope area)
  • Evidence timeliness (days between control event and evidence record)
Speed and responsiveness
  • Time from trigger to decision (end-to-end cycle time)
  • Reporting cycle time (days from data cut to distribution)
  • Escalation response time (time from alert to action)
  • Time to first draft (AI-assisted output available for review)
Appendix E

Responsible AI and control checklist.

Human oversight
  • Human review required before AI outputs are used in decisions or reporting
  • Decision authority clearly assigned to a named individual
  • Override and escalation paths defined and tested
  • No autonomous action without explicit approval
Explainability
  • AI reasoning available for every output in scope
  • Sufficient detail for a regulator or auditor to understand the output
  • Explanation format agreed with Compliance and Legal in advance
Data governance
  • Data use approved by the data owner before processing
  • Personal and sensitive data identified and handled appropriately
  • Retention and deletion policy agreed before proof starts
  • Access controls documented and enforced
Model risk
  • AI model or tool registered in the model inventory
  • Model risk assessment completed before production use
  • Performance monitoring in place from day one of proof
  • Degradation triggers defined (when to suspend and review)
Audit and evidence
  • All AI outputs and human decisions logged with timestamp and reviewer ID
  • Audit trail immutable and accessible to Internal Audit
  • Evidence package produced at end of proof period
  • Findings reported to CRO / CFO at go / adjust / stop gate
