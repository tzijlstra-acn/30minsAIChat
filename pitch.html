02 / 08 · THE BURNING PLATFORM

The Regulatory Moment

Three converging forces are reshaping Non-Financial Risk at European banks.

DORA
In force 17 January 2025

EU Regulation 2022/2554 mandates ICT risk management, incident reporting within 4 hours, TLPT testing and ICT third-party registers for all EU financial entities. Non-compliance exposed to supervisory sanctions under Art.50.

BCMTPRMIS RiskIncident Reporting
EU AI Act
Applies from August 2026 for high-risk AI

Regulation 2024/1689 classifies credit scoring, fraud detection and model-based risk management as high-risk AI systems. Banks must implement conformity assessments, model documentation and human oversight before deployment.

MRMModel GovernanceCompliance
Basel IV / CRR III
Applies from 1 January 2025

The Standardised Measurement Approach replaces the Advanced Measurement Approach for operational risk capital from CRR III. Banks must maintain a 10-year internal loss database and apply the Internal Loss Multiplier (ILM) to their Business Indicator Component.

ORMCapitalRCSA
MaRisk (DE) · BWG (AT) · FINMA Circular 2023/1 (CH) · ECB SREP · EBA Guidelines — add to this a BaFin BAIT update, PSD3, EMIR Refit and the incoming AML package, and the volume of compliance obligations for a DACH bank has reached a tipping point.
