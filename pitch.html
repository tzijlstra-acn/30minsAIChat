Reference · A5

Lean client entry: methodology principles.

P1
Evidence before design
No target state is designed before the diagnostic evidence is assembled. Prevents designing for a context that does not match the actual operating model.
P2
Gate-first, not timeline-first
Each phase has a gate test. The next phase only begins when the gate test passes. Timelines are targets, not commitments.
P3
Lighthouse before platform
Prove value in one controlled use case before investing in platform infrastructure. Lighthouse evidence justifies the platform investment.
P4
Human-centred AI design
Every use case design specifies which decisions remain with humans, which are AI-assisted, and what the escalation triggers are. Not configurable at runtime without governance approval.

Reference · A8

Responsible AI: minimum checklist for risk use cases.

Every AI system deployed in a risk function must satisfy these requirements before going live. Use as a design checklist from the first proof sprint, not as a post-launch compliance form.

Transparency and accountability
Explainability
Every AI decision must produce an explanation satisfying the relevant regulator's standards. Confirm before selecting a model architecture.
Audit trail
All AI decisions logged with timestamp, input hash, output, and model version. The log is immutable and accessible to internal audit and relevant regulators.
Named ownership
Every AI system in production has a named human accountable owner. Ownership is formally documented and survives staff rotation.
User disclosure
People whose outcomes are materially influenced by AI must be notified where applicable regulation requires it. Confirm jurisdiction-specific obligations before launch.
Fairness and robustness
Bias and fairness audit
Training data and model outputs audited for protected-class bias before deployment. Differential performance across groups documented and justified with risk-owner sign-off.
Adversarial testing
System tested for prompt injection, data poisoning, and edge-case inputs before production. Residual risks formally accepted by the accountable owner.
Distributional robustness
Model tested on out-of-distribution inputs. Confidence thresholds defined below which the model escalates to human review rather than deciding autonomously.
Vulnerable populations
Where AI influences decisions affecting vulnerable groups, heightened review is mandatory and a qualified human sign-off is required regardless of automation level.
Human control
Human oversight
Decision authority limits defined before the pilot begins. AI cannot exceed its defined scope without explicit governance approval and a new impact assessment.
Override mechanism
Human operators can always override or reject any AI output. Overrides are logged, reviewed periodically, and used to improve the model or the surrounding process.
Escalation path
A documented and tested route exists for AI failure, uncertainty flags, or edge cases. Escalation paths are exercised at least quarterly and results reviewed by the risk owner.
Monitoring and drift
Model performance tracked continuously in production. Drift alerts trigger human review before any automated retraining. Monitoring is part of the control framework, not optional.
Data and compliance
Data provenance
All training and inference data has documented lineage, a consent basis where required, and a retention policy aligned to applicable law and regulation. No data used without confirmed permission.
Privacy by design
Data minimisation and purpose limitation verified. Personal data in AI pipelines identified before training; a DPIA or equivalent review completed before processing begins.
Model risk governance
Production models registered, validated, and approved per the client's MRM policy. Third-party and open-source models subject to the same governance standards as internally built models.
Regulatory alignment
Jurisdiction-specific AI regulation reviewed by regulatory advisory before deployment in any regulated activity. Applicable rules confirmed with legal counsel; not assumed from public summaries.
This checklist represents minimum standards for Accenture-led AI risk engagements. Jurisdiction, use-case risk tier, and client-specific governance policies may require additional controls. Confirm with Accenture's regulatory advisory practice before deployment in any regulated activity. This page does not constitute legal or regulatory advice.
